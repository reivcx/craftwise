Enchanting

Learn how XP, enchantments and anvils fit into Minecraft progression.

1. Build the setup

An enchanting table is the central block for enchanting gear. Bookshelves can increase the enchantment levels available when the required arrangement is met.

2. Get XP

Experience comes from activities such as mining certain blocks, defeating mobs and smelting. XP levels are spent when enchanting and during some anvil operations.

3. Know the useful effects

Mending repairs an item when you gain XP orbs, while Unbreaking increases durability. Other enchantments are specialized for armor, weapons or tools.

Enchanting exampleEffect
MendingRepairs the item when gaining XP orbs.
UnbreakingIncreases item durability.
Aqua AffinityImproves underwater mining speed.

4. Use an anvil carefully

Anvils can combine compatible enchanted items and books and can rename items. Plan valuable gear instead of repeatedly combining random items.

Official reference: Minecraft Help lists Mending, Unbreaking, Aqua Affinity, Protection and many other enchantments with their effects and applicable item categories.
