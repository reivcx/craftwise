The Nether

Prepare for lava, hostile mobs, Nether resources and getting home safely.

1. Prepare your portal trip

Bring food, blocks, weapons, tools and spare equipment. Make sure you can recognize your route back to the portal.

2. Protect yourself

Wear armor and carry blocks for emergency walls and bridges. Avoid taking irreplaceable items on your first trips.

3. Understand Piglins

Piglins are an important Nether mob and have interactions involving gold. The Nether Update also introduced new Nether biomes, Hoglins and Netherite.

4. Progress toward Netherite

Ancient Debris can be refined into Netherite Scrap, which combines with gold to make Netherite Ingots. Netherite gear is used as an upgrade for high-tier equipment via the smithing system.

5. Get home safely

  • Mark routes consistently.
  • Protect your portal area.
  • Carry emergency food.
  • Record coordinates when useful, especially in Java Edition.
Important: Regular beds are not a normal sleeping solution in the Nether; using one there can cause an explosion.
