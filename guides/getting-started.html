Getting Started

A practical beginner guide to starting a Minecraft Survival world.

1. Gather your first resources

Start by collecting wood from a nearby tree. Turn logs into planks and use four planks to make a crafting table. The crafting table expands your crafting options beyond the small inventory grid.

Easy opening order: logs → planks → crafting table → sticks → wooden pickaxe → stone tools.

2. Upgrade your tools

Use stone as soon as you can to replace wooden tools. A useful early set is a pickaxe, axe, shovel and sword. Keep spare sticks and a little food with you.

3. Secure food

Collect food from animals, crops or other available sources. Cooking food is a useful early priority because reliable food lets you explore and mine for longer.

4. Build a starter base

Your first house does not need to be pretty. A small enclosed room with a door, light, storage and a bed is enough to establish a safe home.

5. Next goals

  • Get iron tools and armor.
  • Craft a shield and bucket.
  • Start a renewable food source.
  • Organize storage.
  • Explore nearby biomes and structures.
