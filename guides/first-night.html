Survive the First Night

Prepare before sunset so hostile mobs don't catch you unprepared.

1. Prepare before sunset

Gather wood, stone and food early. Make a basic weapon and pickaxe, then collect enough blocks to build a shelter quickly.

2. Make a safe shelter

A dirt, stone or wooden starter house is completely fine. The key is to enclose your living space so hostile mobs cannot simply walk inside.

3. Add light

Torches are one of the simplest ways to illuminate your base and nearby areas. Prioritize entrances, work areas and storage.

4. Get a bed

A bed lets you sleep through the night and sets your spawn point when used. Put it somewhere safe inside your base.

5. Plan for morning

  • Smelt food and iron.
  • Expand storage.
  • Start crops or animal breeding.
  • Prepare your first mining trip.
