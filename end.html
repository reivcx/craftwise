The End & Dragon

Prepare for the stronghold, End dimension and Ender Dragon encounter.

1. Find the stronghold

Strongholds contain the portal room used to reach the End. Eyes of Ender are the classic tool for locating a stronghold: they can be thrown to travel toward the nearest stronghold.

2. Prepare your gear

Bring strong armor, a reliable weapon, a ranged option, food and blocks. Enchantments can improve durability and combat capability.

3. Bring supplies

  • Plenty of food.
  • Blocks for protection and movement.
  • Ranged weapon and ammunition.
  • Useful tools and backup equipment.
  • A recovery plan if you die.

4. Fight the dragon

The Ender Dragon is supported by End Crystals on the central island. A common approach is to deal with the crystals first, then attack the dragon during vulnerable moments while watching for knockback and falling hazards.

5. Explore after the fight

Defeating the dragon is a major milestone, but the End has more to explore. The outer islands contain additional structures and resources.
