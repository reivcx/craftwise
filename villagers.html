Villagers & Trading

Use professions, workstations and trading to build a useful resource network.

1. Understand professions

Villagers can have professions that determine their available trades. Workstations are important to how professions and work behavior function.

2. Common professions

ProfessionCommon reason players use it
LibrarianBook-related trades
FarmerCrop and food-related trades
Armorer / Toolsmith / WeaponsmithEquipment-related trades

3. Trading

Trades use emeralds and specified items. Exact mechanics and trade pools can change between editions and versions, so check your version before building a large trading hall.

4. Build a safe trading area

  • Keep villagers separated so their workstations are easy to understand.
  • Light the area.
  • Protect villagers from hostile mobs.
  • Label useful villagers.

5. Protect your villagers

Villagers are vulnerable. Keep them away from caves, cliffs and hostile mobs and make their work area controlled and easy to access.
