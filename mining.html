Mining & Resources

Prepare properly, choose a sensible mining method and manage underground hazards.

1. Prepare

Bring a pickaxe, food, torches, blocks for emergencies and a way to deal with lava. A spare pickaxe is useful on longer trips.

2. Use the right tool

ToolTypical use
PickaxeStone, ores and many underground blocks
ShovelDirt, gravel, sand and similar blocks
AxeWood and many wooden blocks

3. Choose a method

Caves expose many blocks quickly, while controlled tunnels give you predictable movement. Your choice should depend on the resource you need and your current Minecraft version.

4. Stay safe

  • Light explored routes.
  • Be cautious when opening unknown spaces.
  • Watch for lava and deep drops.
  • Return home when your inventory becomes difficult to manage.

5. Sort resources

Keep ores, building blocks, mob drops and tools in predictable storage locations. Good organization makes every future mining trip faster.
